About Vougenest

Vougenest began in 2019 in a small Durban studio with one idea: luxury should feel effortless.

Our founder, S'bahle Maphumulo, trained in tailoring before moving into design. She kept finding that beautiful clothes were often uncomfortable, and comfortable clothes were rarely beautiful. Vougenest was her answer to both. Let the quality run through her before the masses

What we stand for

We represent in black, gold and silver because they never date. Our collections are small, our barnds are trustworthy

Our promise

Clothes that look expensive, feel easy and stay in your wardrobe for years.
