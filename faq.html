Frequently asked questions

How do I view an item's details?

Open the Catalogue page and select a category. Each piece shows its name, type and price.

Can I buy online?

Not yet. This website is a showcase. Visit our boutique or contact us to enquire about a piece.

Where is the boutique?

93 Chiltern Dr, Clare Estate, Durban. Open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm.

Do you offer custom sizing?

Yes. Our tailoring team can adjust any piece. Contact us to book a fitting.

How do I care for my garments?

Each item has a care label. As a rule, dry clean silks and wools, and store leather away from direct sun.

Do you ship internationally?

Not at the moment, but we are planning to open international orders soon.
